Who we are

UC BAZZAR is a small, independently run game top-up store for Indian players. We started in 2023 as a side project between a few friends who were tired of waiting hours for UC — and of losing money to unverified sellers on social media. Today we deliver BGMI UC and Free Fire MAX Diamonds through a simple three-step checkout, and every single order is handled by a real person.

What we deliver

  • BGMI UC — from the ₹199 Elite Pass pack up to 8100 UC, for every BGMI UID.
  • Free Fire MAX Diamonds — 2400 to 10500 Diamonds, credited straight to your UID.
  • UPI payments only — scan the QR in checkout or tap GPay / PhonePe / Paytm / BHIM UPI. The amount is filled in automatically, so you never type the price yourself.

Our promise

  • No carding, no chargebacks. Every top-up comes from a legitimate channel, so your game ID is never at risk of a ban.
  • No password, no OTP. We only ever ask for your Player ID / UID. Anyone asking for more is a scammer.
  • Manual verification. We check the payment before delivering, which is why delivery lands within minutes and not "someday".
  • Human support. A real person answers your email — no bots, no ticket queues that go nowhere.
