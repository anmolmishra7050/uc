Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. What we collect

  • Player ID / UID — used only to deliver your top-up.
  • Order details — package, amount and payment reference.
  • Contact details — your email address, if you contact our support.

2. What we never collect

We never ask for your game password, OTP, bank login or card details. Anyone who asks for them is committing fraud — report it to us immediately.

3. Payments

Payments go from your UPI app directly to our UPI ID. We never have access to your UPI PIN or bank credentials.

4. Data sharing

Your Player ID is shared only with the official top-up channel, and only to deliver your order. We never sell or rent your data to any third party.

5. Storage

Your Player ID may be saved in your browser to make repeat orders faster. You can remove it any time by clearing your browser data.

6. Cookies

The website uses local storage for basic functionality only. No tracking ad networks are integrated.

7. Contact

Questions? Visit the contact page or email us.
